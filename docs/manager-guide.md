# People Action Check — Manager Guide

For managers using the Slack app. Read this once before your first check.

## What this is

A 5-question risk check you run before taking action on an employee situation — a performance conversation, attendance issue, possible termination, anything HR-adjacent. It tells you if the situation is Low, Elevated, or High risk, and gives you next steps. It does not replace HR — it tells you when to involve them.

## Starting a check

Two ways to start:

1. Type `/pac` in any Slack channel or DM.
2. Or open the People Action Check app, go to the **Home** tab, click **Start New Check**.

Either way, a form opens.

## Step 1: Pick the scenario

Select one or more situations that match what you're dealing with — Performance Decline, Attendance Issue, Termination Consideration, and so on. If you pick more than one, the questions come from the first (primary) one you picked, but all of them get recorded.

## Step 2: Private self-check, or maybe escalate to HR?

There's one more field on this screen: a short text box.

- **Leave it blank** if you're just checking for yourself right now — you don't know yet if this is going anywhere. Your result stays private. There's no button to notify HR. Nobody else sees this.
- **Type anything** (your initials, a short code, whatever you'll recognize later) if there's a real chance you'll need HR. This doesn't send anything to HR yet — it just unlocks the option, and gives you something to search for later. HR never sees what you typed here.

If you're not sure which to pick: type something. You can decide not to notify HR later, but if you leave it blank, you can't add HR into the loop without starting over.

Click **Continue**.

## Step 3: Answer 5 questions

Each question is Yes / No / Don't Know. Answer honestly — the result is only useful if it reflects reality, not what you wish were true.

Some questions are marked ⚠️. Answering No or Don't Know on one of those sends you straight to High Risk, regardless of the other answers — these are the questions where getting it wrong has real legal exposure (documentation, prior warnings, leave/accommodation factors).

Click **See Result**.

## Step 4: Your result

You'll get a DM from the app with:

- Your risk level: 🟢 Low, 🟡 Elevated, or 🔴 High
- A plain-language summary of what that means
- 2–3 recommended next steps specific to your scenario

If you left the case field blank in Step 2, that's it — this DM is private, no other buttons appear.

If you filled it in, you'll also see two buttons:

- **Notify HR** — sends this case to HR for review. On High Risk cases this button is red — stop and use it before you do anything else with the employee.
- **Upload Documentation** — attach PIPs, write-ups, or other records to this case, before or after notifying HR.

## After you notify HR

HR will acknowledge the case, and may follow up with a question or ask for more information — you'll get a DM when they do, with a **Reply** button to respond right there in Slack. You'll also see status updates as they work the case (Under Review, Escalated, Resolved, Closed).

## Where to find your past checks

Open the People Action Check app, **Home** tab. You'll see your 5 most recent checks with their risk level and status. For full history, use **My Cases** from the `/pac` menu, or open the web app.

## Quick answers

**Can I redo a check?** Yes — just run `/pac` again. Each check is independent.

**Does HR see my private self-checks?** No. HR only ever sees cases where you typed something in that self-check-or-escalate field and clicked Notify HR.

**What if I pick High Risk by mistake?** Nothing is locked in — you can still choose not to notify HR. But if you got a High Risk result, read the next steps first; there's usually a real reason for it.

**Can I export my cases?** Yes, from the `/pac` menu — click **Export**. HR can export across all managers.
